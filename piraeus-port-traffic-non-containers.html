Relative-magnitude restriction

Post-treatment violations are bounded by M̄ times the largest pre-treatment violation.

Smoothness restriction

M bounds changes in the slope of the untreated Piraeus–control difference.

The conventional average of the post-treatment event coefficients is +18.5%. At zero allowed relative-magnitude deviation, the HonestDiD confidence set does not include zero.

HonestDiD asks how much the post-treatment violation of parallel trends could exceed the observed pre-treatment deviations. Intervals that include zero mean the sign is not robust to that allowance; the analysis is a sensitivity calculation, not a new point estimator.
