Interpretation

This stacked design prevents already-treated Thessaloniki or Heraklio from acting as untreated controls. With only five original port clusters—and only two never-treated ports—its p-values and confidence intervals remain fragile.

Method and scope

Outcome construction: container tonnage is the sum of external/internal loaded/unloaded container cargo. Non-container tonnage equals total cargo minus container cargo.

Seasonality: recurring port-specific quarterly patterns are absorbed with port × quarter-of-year fixed effects. Calendar-quarter fixed effects absorb shocks common to the included ports.

Identification: the all-port models require credible counterfactual trends across ports; the within-Piraeus model instead requires container and non-container traffic to have comparable untreated evolution. Neither assumption is guaranteed.

Transition treatment: the August 2016 transfer makes 2016 Q3 partially treated. It is excluded everywhere, with 2016 Q2 retained as the last fully untreated quarter and 2016 Q4 as the first full post-treatment quarter.

Small-cluster warning: several clustered event-study covariance matrices required a numerical positive-semidefinite correction. With only three to five ports in the donor specifications, confidence intervals and p-values should be treated as descriptive sensitivity measures rather than definitive inference.
